About

A versatile analyst who cares about the parts that make analysis trustworthy.

I'm a Data & Business Analyst currently at the University of Illinois, with earlier experience delivering regulated production releases for a top-10 U.S. consumer lender at EXL. I'm not tied to one industry — I've worked across finance, lending, higher-ed, healthcare, and marketing — because the core skill travels: start from a business question, get the data right, and land on a recommendation someone can act on.

I work across the whole pipeline: pulling and cleaning genuinely messy data, expressing logic in SQL that ports from DuckDB to Snowflake on an S3 landing zone, validating at scale, modeling in Python / scikit-learn, and building Tableau and Streamlit front ends so the work doesn't die in a notebook.

I lean toward interpretable models and explicit guardrails — logistic regression with odds ratios when a team needs the why, read-only, schema-scoped SQL when an LLM is near a database. In regulated work I've built dual-review audit frameworks and reconciliation pipelines because, there, being right the first time isn't optional.
